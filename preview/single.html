Shipping a WordPress theme with Tailwind in 2026

A practical walkthrough of wiring Tailwind CSS v4 into a classic WordPress theme.

Ada Byron
October 18, 2026 · 6 min read

The first thing people notice about a Medium-style theme is not the color palette — it is the typography. Readers arrive for the words, so the layout steps back and lets long-form text breathe. This fixture exists to prove the reading experience holds up at every viewport.

Start with the content column

A constrained measure of roughly sixty-five characters keeps lines comfortable. Everything else — navigation, metadata, related posts — arranges itself around that single decision.

Good design is as little design as possible. Less, but better — because it concentrates on the essential aspects.
